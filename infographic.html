Uniquely Human Roles

  • Intent & Problem Framing: Defining what needs to be built and why.
  • Critical Evaluation: Spotting subtle errors, hallucinations, and logic bugs.
  • Contextual Ethics: Ensuring solutions fit human needs and moral standards.

Delegated AI Roles

  • Syntax Generation: Writing boilerplate code and repetitive functions.
  • Pattern Synthesis: Fetching examples from massive training sets.
  • Rapid Scaffolding: Turning prompts into initial first-draft prototypes.
